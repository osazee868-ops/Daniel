ACTIVE BRAINS ACADEMY

About the School

ACTIVE BRAINS ACADEMY – igniting brains for excellence Founded in 2022, Active Brains Academy is more than a school – it is the fulfillment of a vision rooted in passion, purpose, and the pursuit of excellence. Our mission is to nurture young minds, build character, and prepare students to thrive academically and socially in a changing world. But to truly understand Active Brains Academy, one must first know the heart behind it. The Founder: Engr. Mrs. Uzebu Nnenna Janet With over 13 years of teaching experience, Engr. Mrs. Uzebu Nnenna Janet is the backbone and visionary behind Active Brains Academy. Her journey began not in a classroom, but in her own home – with a deep desire to see children succeed in their academics. While her children attended Brother Pius School, she began helping them and neighboring children prepare for exams. What started as a simple act of service quickly grew into a community norm. After completing her NYSC, she embraced this calling fully, becoming a dedicated home lesson teacher. Her commitment and impact led to the creation of Active Brains Tutorials, a lecture center that became a hub for academic excellence. From this foundation, Active Brains Academy was born - a full-fledged secondary school built on the values of discipline, excellence and personalized learning.

OUR GROWTH:

At Active Brains Academy, growth means action. We focus on practical learning, not just theory-students build, experiment, and solve real problems. With a strong emphasis on STEM and innovation, we train young minds to think creatively and work with technology. Our passionate educators and vibrant community makes sure every child thrives.

OUR PHILOSOPHY:

We believe that every child is capable of greatness when given the right tools, guidance and environment. At Active Brains Academy, we don’t just teach-we inspire, mentor, and build futures.
